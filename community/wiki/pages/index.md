# ExpanseMUD Wiki

A community reference for Expanse, a fantasy civilization simulation RPG in active development.

The wiki explains the worlds, gameplay systems, community, and creator tools using documented releases. Because the game is evolving, verify details against your installed game version.

## Start here

- [Getting started](getting-started.html) — Install Expanse, begin a single-player adventure and manage saves.
- [People and the living world](living-world.html) — NPC interactions, jobs, animals and singing.
- [Magic and combat](magic-and-combat.html) — Spells, effects and character abilities.
- [Creator tools](creator-tools.html) — Edit, export and share world content.
- [Multiplayer development](multiplayer.html) — What is available now and what remains in development.
- [Community guidelines](community-guidelines.html) — Participation and moderation.
