# Getting started

Expanse offers a personal single-player adventure and Creator tools. The shared online MMO is not yet enabled for public play.

## Install on your computer

Visit [expansemud.com](https://expansemud.com/) for the current Windows 11 and Linux/Bazzite installers. Download the installer appropriate to your system, follow the included instructions, and let the installer update an existing Expanse installation in place.

Do not delete existing game folders, custom worlds, saves or account data when updating. Windows and Linux packages are intended as in-place updates, not new applications.

## Begin an adventure

Launch Expanse and choose Single Player. Open the installed Wild Lands world and create a character, or resume a compatible previously saved character.

## Saving

Save your single-player progress manually when you want to keep it. Do not assume that closing a window automatically preserves recent changes. Check your current release's Save and Load controls.

## Online play

The hosted online service currently provides account-isolated Single Player worlds. Shared MMO play is in development and stays unavailable until security, authorization, world persistence and cross-account tests pass.

## Need assistance?

Until the new community forum launches, visit the [existing community forum](https://expansemud.discourse.group/) for help. Include your game version and platform when you report a problem, but never post login credentials or recovery codes.
