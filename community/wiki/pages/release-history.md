# Release history

All dates are US Central. “Official game” and “official forum” mark successful deployments. A “test package” was shared privately, and a “build” marks work recorded on that date. The dates for these packages and builds establish when testers had them or when they were made. The surviving records begin on November 6. Where they give only an app version or only a world version, that is the version listed here.

## November 6, 2026

Linux/Bazzite test package. World Systems expanded residents’ buying and barter. Shops gained opening hours and residents followed work schedules.

## November 7

Local builds. Four development builds worked on world coordinates, the Full Atlas, playability, routes and the calendar.

## November 9

Linux/Bazzite test package. Fresh offline adventures started at 06:15. Players could save manually or quit without saving.

## November 10

Linux/Bazzite test packages. Wayfarer Saves and Open Horizons added Resident Watch, visible weather, click-to-walk travel, escort actions and borders on the globe.

## November 11

Android and Linux/Bazzite test packages. Version 1.4 separated the Android play, map and character screens. Versions 1.5 through 1.5.5 improved sound controls, map centering, lighting, weather and zoom. The Wild Lands expansion arrived in 1.6. Versions 1.7 through 1.7.2 improved portable worlds, doorway controls and walking. With app and world 1.8.0, players could find their first companion through the Adventurers’ Pet Union.

## November 12

Android and Linux/Bazzite test packages. Versions 1.8.1 through 1.8.4 stabilized map coordinates, clarified quest instructions, repaired farmhouse roofs and filled in blank atlas surroundings. Wild Lands 1.9.0 added scheduled day and night city patrols.

## November 13

Official game. The first successful hosted deployment in these records included Creator tools for checking world data.

Android and Linux/Bazzite test packages. App 1.9.1 corrected missing ground at a trailhead and buildings displayed as grass. It continued to use Wild Lands 1.9.0.

## November 14

Official game. Players received feedback when damage occurred during play.

## November 15

Official game. Residents gained conversation choices about work, plans, creatures, local news and memories. Updates also improved character-name editing, walking controls and labels on the world atlas.

World packages. Wild Lands 1.9.8 linked books, people and places to local quest paths. Version 1.9.9 made the first quest easier to find and accept.

## November 16

Official game. Updates improved character paper-doll colors and Creator support for multi-stop quests. World authors could add songbooks and performances. Following a route became more reliable when the character’s location changed.

World packages. Wild Lands 1.9.11 added usable containers and updated quests and routes. Version 1.9.12 added music and instruments. Version 1.9.14 offered a starter quest as an alternative to the interface guide. Version 1.9.15 made magic more visible in the setting.

## November 17

Official game. Players could see which playtest account they had signed into. Each account had its own character and progress.

World build and desktop test packages. Wild Lands 1.9.16 was built. A separate Linux/Bazzite and Windows 11 desktop package, app 1.9.17.1, included a playable world and a quest fix. The records do not identify that package’s world version.

## November 18

Official game. Browser and desktop clients gained support for reusing unchanged game media between visits.

Linux/Bazzite and Windows 11 test packages. Desktop 1.9.17.2 bundled Wild Lands 1.9.15. A tester continued an existing Linux save in this version.

## November 19

Official game. World authors could add festivals that players could observe. Map selections identified both the selected place and the character’s current location. The map distinguished stacked terrain and offered fixed and following camera modes.

Official forum. Category descriptions explained what belonged in each section and whether it was open for posts. The sign-in page showed a registration option while registration was open. Older forum bookmarks continued to work after a move.

Linux/Bazzite and Windows 11 test packages. Desktop 1.9.18.1 bundled Wild Lands 1.9.16. Players could lock the map, pan it and switch back to following their character. A second Linux package fixed launching from the application menu.

## November 20

Official game. Players could open the wiki and forum from the game and use Expanse emotes in the interface. Quest Compass lookups and map refreshes became smoother. Recent dialogue remained available during play.

Android, Linux/Bazzite and Windows 11 test packages. App 1.9.18.2 added wiki and community access and custom emotes. The Android delivery included a signed update for existing installations. App 1.9.18.3 improved Quest Compass and large-map responsiveness and reused matching artwork and sounds. Offline terminal play regained manual saving. The bundled world remained Wild Lands 1.9.16.

## November 21

Official game and forum. NPC conversations used nearby residents and their displayed names. An Expanse emoji picker appeared in the forum’s topic and reply forms.

## November 22

Version 1.10.0 build. Founding Cities opens with 3,000 registered residents in each of eight cities. Players can visit households, read the daily food accounts, and take a funded kitchen or market job when work and sponsored lodging are available.

The build includes graphical and terminal editions for Windows 11 and Linux, a signed Android app, a separate world archive and the online server package. The terminal editions include their runtime for offline play. This date records the verified build. Public deployment is tracked separately.
