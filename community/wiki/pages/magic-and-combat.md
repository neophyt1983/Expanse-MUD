# Magic and combat

Combat, weapons, spells and visible magical effects are part of Expanse's fantasy RPG systems. Skill access depends on class, ancestry, level, equipment and game-world rules.

## Learning magic

The 1.9.15 magic release spotlight documents a curriculum of 66 spells. That does not mean every character can learn all 66 spells: eligibility, training, cooldowns, mana, consumables and other requirements apply.

## Visible effects

Spell effects can be drawn in the game interface. Genuine renderer previews are distinct from live gameplay captures, and the actual appearance and behavior vary by build and available world content.

## Combat choices

Spells may attack, protect, heal, repair, illuminate, summon temporary companions or assist travel. Combat and world rules remain authoritative on the host; copied graphics do not create abilities or grant privileges.
