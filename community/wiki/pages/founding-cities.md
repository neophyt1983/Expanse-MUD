# Visit a Founding Cities household

Start a new single-player adventure and choose **Wild Lands — Founding Cities**. Each of the eight cities has an opening register of 3,000 named residents. On desktop, use the Founding Cities template selected by the 1.10.0 installer for a fresh start. If your host offers several world versions, choose the one marked **1.10.0**. To resume an earlier character, open its existing save and world.

## Find your first door


- From a city market, enter `go housing lane` to reach the first residential court.
- Enter `read Court Roll` for a door number and the names of the people registered there.
- Enter `knock 2` to call at door two. To ask for one person, enter `knock 2`, a space, and their full name as printed on the roll. At Dawnwatch Orchard Court 1, try `knock 2 Morgan Oakwell`.

A resident who answers appears in the court and can be spoken to there. Someone who has moved away or died may be unavailable. Use `go onward` to explore the next court where that exit is listed, or `go back` to retrace your steps.

## Who answers when you return?

After you leave, an idle resident can return home. Their name, wallet and belongings stay with them. Go back to their court and knock at the same door to meet them again. Residents under an active watch or escort may still be out, and a resident who has died stays on the register as part of the city’s history.

## Try a first job

Ask at the kitchen or food market during local business hours with `startwork status`. Staff need stock and funds to offer a contract. Pick a room from the available quotes and enter the full acceptance command shown.


- **Cook** — Start six servings of vegetable stew with `startwork cook`. Preparation takes 30 active game minutes. Check the order again, then use `startwork deliver` at the kitchen when it is ready.
- **Deliver** — Take a delivery order at the market, go to its named inn, enter `startwork collect` and carry the six portions back to the market. Enter `startwork deliver` there.

A completed order pays 30 gold. If you accepted sponsored lodging, the sponsor pays the quoted rent separately. Go to the named public parcel and use `startwork check-in`. The room lasts nine active game days.

## Read the daily food account

Enter `provisions` for your city or `provisions Reedwater` to look up another. At 15:00 each game day, the co-operative serves registered offscreen households first, issuing up to two portions per resident. It can store up to four days of meals.

Active, off-duty adults can walk to an open city kitchen or market and collect a prepared meal while portions last. Work shifts, closed counters or an unavailable route can keep a resident from collecting one. Eating the meal reduces hunger.

Follow the food from field to kitchen to counter. The account shows the harvest, portions cooked, portions issued, the shortfall, deliveries to the market and meals left in reserve. It also shows recent shortages. At the local market, enter `news /all` to read the provision counter’s dispatch alongside other news.

## Read the city’s history

Visit the public library to see when each court opened. In Dawnwatch, enter `read Dawnwatch Founding Register`. Elsewhere, use the city name printed on the book. The register records the opening population. Your encounters reflect what has happened since your adventure began.

## Return to your adventure

Save your single-player adventure before quitting. Continuing the same adventure preserves the residents you met. Starting a new adventure creates a separate world.
