# Creator tools

Expanse includes a Creator/editor workflow for modifying worlds, characters, NPCs, equipment, locations and authored content. Exports are central to the project's goal of allowing others to share creations.

## Creator and Dungeon Master concepts

An offline single-player experience may grant you substantial control over your own world. A shared world requires a host and permissions; not every player may edit its authoritative state.

## Sharing worlds

Exported content can be shared using supported game files and editor features. Verify the game and engine versions expected by a recipient before publishing a package.

## Shared MMO permissions

The planned MMO roles are No_Edit, Admin, and Full_Edit. These roles must be enforced by the server and are not a guarantee that shared MMO is currently available. No_Edit is the ordinary player's planned classification.

## Community showcase
