# People and the living world

Expanse is designed as a civilization simulation in which NPCs and the world have daily activities beyond combat and quest-giving. Individual systems continue to be expanded and tested.

## Conversations and society

Residents can speak with players and one another. Their work, routines and relationships are parts of Expanse's developing simulation. Conversations can affect what you discover and how you understand a settlement.

## Music and singing

People can sing in Expanse. Musical actions and performances are part of the authored game, not a promise of recorded human singing or a fully produced soundtrack. Actual behavior depends on the installed release and content.

## Farms, wildlife and zoos

The world includes farms and diverse animal life. Zoos may include dangerous or unusual creatures and monsters. Encounters, animal routines and population behavior vary with the world and current game content.

## Editor changes

The creator can alter content and export worlds for others. Treat shared world descriptions as content from the relevant author and version, not necessarily a guarantee about every server.
