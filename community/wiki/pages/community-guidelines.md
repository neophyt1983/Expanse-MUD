# Community guidelines

The Expanse community exists to discuss the game and its development in a lively but respectful way.

- Be courteous. Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
- Keep bug reports specific: platform, version, steps to reproduce and expected result.
- Mark speculation, suggestions and incomplete features clearly.
- Protect private information. Do not post passwords, account recovery codes, personal addresses or private logs.
- No spam, harassment, scams, impersonation or malicious links.
- Share your own work or properly attributed material. Do not pass generated graphics off as screenshots of functioning gameplay.
- Official News & Server Status is reserved for authorized Expanse staff announcements.

## Moderation

Staff may lock discussions, suspend accounts and review reports. The forum prototype uses moderator-approved registrations during its initial testing phase. More complete appeal, account recovery, privacy and retention controls must be reviewed before open public deployment.
